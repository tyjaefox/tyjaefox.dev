Privacy Policy

Last updated — 22 Aug 2026Applies to Heron for Windows

1. Overview

Heron ("we," "us," or "our") is built with privacy as a foundational principle. This Privacy Policy applies to the Heron application available via the Microsoft Store.

2. Zero Data Collection

We do not collect, transmit, or access any personal data or usage information. If you choose to include personal details in your own notes, that content is stored locally on your device only — see 'Local Storage Only' below — and is never transmitted to us or anyone else.

No Personal Data: Heron does not require account creation, registration, or any personal details (such as names, email addresses, or phone numbers) to function.

No Telemetry or Tracking: We do not collect app usage metrics, analytics, diagnostics, crash reports, or device identifiers.

No Location Data: Heron does not access, track, or share your precise or approximate geographical location.

Local Storage Only: Any preferences, settings, or files created or modified within Heron remain strictly stored on your local device and are never transmitted to external servers.

3. Microsoft Platform Data

Heron is distributed through the Microsoft Store. Any transaction data, licensing checks, or system-level download analytics are managed entirely by Microsoft in accordance with the Microsoft Privacy Statement. We do not have access to or control over Microsoft's system logs or store processing data.

4. Third-Party Services

Heron does not integrate any third-party analytics, advertising networks, tracking SDKs, or external APIs that process user data.

5. Changes to This Policy

Should we ever update Heron to include features that interact with network services or handle user data, this policy will be updated prior to the deployment of those features.
